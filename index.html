How it plays

A few quiet minutes, one field at a time

  • Tap, cross, place

    Tap a cell to cross it out. Tap a cross to place a hive. Drag to cross out a run of cells.

  • Three bees per field

    A hive in the wrong cell sends a bee away. Lose all three and the bees move on: start the field again.

  • An Almanac when you are stuck

    It explains the next deduction in plain words, three pages a day, free.

  • Logic, never luck

    Every field has exactly one solution, and you can reach it by reasoning alone. Fields run from 5×5 to 10×10 and take one to five minutes.

The rules

Four lines, then the field is yours

  1. Every plot gets exactly one hive.

  2. No two hives share a row or a column.

  3. Hives never touch, not even at the corners.

  4. The twistA stone shows how many hives touch it.

    2

Stones sit in the field like the numbers in Minesweeper: they hold no hive themselves, and they settle questions the other rules leave open.
