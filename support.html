How to play

  1. Every plot gets exactly one hive.
  2. No two hives share a row or a column.
  3. Hives never touch, not even at the corners.
  4. A stone shows how many hives touch it.

Tap a cell to cross it out. Tap a cross to place a hive. Drag to cross out a run of cells. Undo takes back crosses, not hives. Each field starts with 3 bees: a wrong hive sends one away, and with none left, the bees move on and you start the field again. The Almanac explains the next step, 3 pages a day. A Smoker places one right hive for you, and a Spare Bee calls a lost bee back. The full rules are behind the “?” button in the game.

Purchases

Spring Fields (40 fields) and today's field are free. Summer Fields, Autumn Fields and Winter Fields are one-time unlocks of 40 fields each, and the Beekeeper's Kit adds 10 Smokers and 5 Spare Bees each time you buy it. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
