Privacy Policy

Skepfield · last updated 28 October 2026

Skepfield is a puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the fields you have solved in each season and the days you solved today's field, with your best time for each and whether it was a Clean solve (no bee lost).
  • Your tools: how many Smokers and Spare Bees you have, how many Almanac pages you have used today, and which tool rewards you have already received.
  • Your settings: whether auto-cross is on.
  • Whether you have bought Summer Fields, Autumn Fields or Winter Fields, and the ids of purchase transactions already delivered, so a purchase is never credited twice.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • No network use, except the App Store when you buy or restore a purchase.

Purchases

Spring Fields (40 fields) and today's field are free. Summer Fields, Autumn Fields and Winter Fields are one-time unlocks, and the Beekeeper's Kit (10 Smokers and 5 Spare Bees) can be bought more than once. Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
